FAQs

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign Up" in the menu, enter your username, full name, email and a password, tick "I accept Terms and Conditions", then click the Sign Up button.

How do I log in?

Click "Log In" in the menu and enter your email and password.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the Log In page, click "Forgot password" and follow the steps to reset it using your email address.

How do I order medicine online?

Search for your medicine or browse the shop, add it to your cart, then go to checkout and confirm your delivery address and payment method.

Do I need a prescription?

Over-the-counter products can be ordered without one. Prescription medicines need a valid prescription, which you can upload from the home page.

How do I upload my prescription?

Click "Upload Prescription" on the home page and attach a clear photo or scan. Our pharmacists will check it before your order is prepared.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. You will see the estimated delivery time at checkout.

What payment methods are accepted?

You can pay by debit or credit card, or choose cash on delivery.

Can I cancel or change my order?

You can cancel or change an order before it is dispatched. After that, please contact us for help.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

The product page shows its availability. If an item is out of stock, you can ask to be notified or choose a similar product.

Can I return a medicine?

Medicines can only be returned if they arrive damaged, expired or incorrect. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

Is my personal and medical information safe?

Yes. Your details and prescriptions are stored securely and are only used to process your orders.
